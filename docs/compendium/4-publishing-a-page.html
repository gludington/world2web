Once a journal is set to publish, each of its pages has publishing controls in the journal's page list:

  • Upload icon: the page is not published. Click it to publish the page.

  • Checkmark: the page is published (see below for what clicking it does).

  • Gear icon: opens Post Settings for that one page, where you can give it its own author, tags, or a front image (a featured image shown with the post).

The color tells you whether the site is up to date:

  • Orange: you've changed something that isn't on the site yet.

  • Green: the site matches what you see in Foundry.

After you edit a published page, its checkmark turns orange. Click it once to republish your changes, then use Publish to Web. Clicking a green checkmark unpublishes the page instead.

Only text and image pages can be published. Next: @UUID[.RCsd666cT4PHmxUS]{Publishing!}.
