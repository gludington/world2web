World2Web publishes your Foundry journals to a website, so you and your players can read lore, quest notes or session recaps even when your Foundry server is offline. Players can even post session notes blog-style, with their character as the author.

See it in action: the World2Web documentation site is this same journal, published from Foundry with World2Web's default settings.

Requirements

You will need two free accounts:

  1. GitHub, where your site's content is stored. The repository is yours: World2Web sends your content straight from Foundry to GitHub, and no World2Web server ever sees it. GitHub also keeps a full history of every change.

  2. A hosting provider, which turns that content into a public website. World2Web has one-click setup for three providers, all with free tiers that are plenty for a campaign site:

    • Cloudflare

    • Netlify

    • Vercel

Any host that can serve plain HTML will work, if you'd rather set one up yourself.

Updates

Your site's design and features come from World2Web itself. When you update the module in Foundry, your site picks up the matching version the next time you publish, with nothing to do on GitHub or at your host.

A note on privacy

Your site is public: anyone with the address can read whatever you publish. Only pages you explicitly publish ever leave Foundry, and nothing is published by default.
