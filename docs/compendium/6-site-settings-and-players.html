Site settings

These are in Configure Settings → World2Web, and take effect the next time you click Publish to Web:

  • Site Name: your site's title, shown in the browser tab and at the top of every page. Defaults to "World2Web".

  • Site Theme: the site's look. Leave it blank for the default, or enter one of the built-in themes: dnd, ravenloft or scifi.

  • Allow Visitor Theme Override: lets visitors choose a different theme for themselves.

  • Keep Site Up to Date: on by default. Each publish updates your site to the version matching your installed World2Web. Leave it on unless you maintain your site's code yourself (see below).

  • Journals URL Segment: the word used in journal addresses, as in yoursite.com/<world>/journals/.... Defaults to "journals".

Homepage Links

Click Edit Links next to Homepage Links to add links shown in a footer on your site's home pages, such as a Discord invite or your campaign's wiki. Give each link a name and a full address starting with https://; use the buttons to add and remove rows.

Links belong to the world they're set in. Each world's home page shows its own links. If several worlds publish to the same site, the site's main homepage shows all of their links together, each address once.

Letting players publish

By default only the Game Master can publish. Turn on Allow Player Self-Publish to let players publish journals they own (Foundry's Owner permission). They get the same gear icons and publish controls you do, but only on their own journals. They can publish whenever they like; no GM needs to be online.

A common setup: give each player a journal they own, in a shared folder such as "Session Notes". Their posts then appear on the site under their character's name and portrait.

Customizing your site further

For advanced users. Your site's repository holds only your content and a few lines of configuration; its pages and styles come from the @world2web/site package. To change the site's code yourself, copy that package's source into your repository and turn off Keep Site Up to Date, so publishing stops changing it. From then on, updating your site is up to you. See the World2Web README for details.
