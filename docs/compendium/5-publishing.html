Publishing a page marks it as ready; this last step sends your changes to the web. At the top of the Journals sidebar tab there is a Publish to Web button:

Orange means there are changes waiting to go out; green means everything is up to date. When you click it:

  1. Every published page, plus any you've unpublished, is sent to your GitHub repository. The first time, or with many images, this can take a while, so please be patient.

  2. Your hosting provider (from @UUID[.YJgINFmlwzaLHjbo]{Module Setup}) notices the change and rebuilds your site. Your posts appear at your site's address a few minutes later.

Publishing also keeps your site in step with your installed version of World2Web: after you update the module, the next publish updates your site to match, and you'll see a notification saying so.

If something goes wrong, see @UUID[.TROUBLESHOOTING_ID]{Troubleshooting}.

Happy publishing!
